Risk-index ranks and their 95% intervals

Each bar is the 95% interval of a unit's rank over the Monte Carlo draws; the dot is the median rank (the top rank is the highest index). Units whose intervals overlap should not be read as different. The intervals are approximate in both directions: they carry Poisson-type and sampling error only, but they draw neighbouring units' rainfall slopes independently, which overstates rank changes.

§ heatwave-day hazard component is a structural zero (no heatwave day in any year). Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep are NA by rule (no rainfall hazard) and are not ranked.

Early warning: do ENSO forecasts help?

There is no clear ENSO-attributable forecast skill for either outcome: archived forecasts, and even the observed ONI, did not clearly beat a climatological ENSO value. For recorded heat deaths, the gain over a model without ENSO terms is consistent with the Indian Ocean Dipole term, and at the June and September issues it is not lead-time skill.
